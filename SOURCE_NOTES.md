# Fathi-Tech website

This is the complete static website for Fathi-Tech Solutions Limited. It uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with local images and fonts. No build step or package installation is required.

## Source and content

The company profile supplied by the owner, “Fathi-Company Profile 2026.docx”, is the principal source for this update. The horizontal logo and banner illustration are extracted directly from its embedded pages. The standalone original logo is retained in `dist/assets/fathi-tech-original-logo.png`. The four management photographs are also from the profile. No logo was recreated or recoloured.

The profile supplies the expanded services, management profiles, business experience, programme participation, Abuja address, additional phone number, and Gmail address. The primary domain email remains the one published on the original company website. The NITDA electronic waste entry remains explicitly a proposal. Interface artwork is clearly marked as illustrative. No unpublished performance metrics or testimonials have been added.

## Files

- `dist/index.html`: the website, copy, page structure, and contact details.
- `dist/styles.css`: responsive design and typography.
- `dist/app.js`: detail dialogs, project filters, mobile menu, and enquiry preparation.
- `dist/assets/`: all logo, image, and font files.

## Contact form

The form prepares a reviewable email draft. Visitors send it through their own email application, or copy the draft into their email service. The website does not send or store form submissions. Email, telephone, and LinkedIn links are direct visitor actions.

## Hosting

Upload the contents of `dist/` to the root folder of a static web host. Keep `assets/`, `styles.css`, and `app.js` beside `index.html`. No database or backend is required. The Sites hosting configuration is retained separately in `.openai/hosting.json` for this hosted project.

## Validation
